- Link: https://www.frontiersin.org/articles/10.3389/fpsyg.2021.700664/pdf
- Resumo: In this research, the effect of face loss on impulsive buying is examined under the background of Chinese culture. Using experimental studies, we examined the mediating effect of emotion and the moderating effect of self-control. For individuals high in self-control, face loss has no significant impact on their impulsive consumption.
